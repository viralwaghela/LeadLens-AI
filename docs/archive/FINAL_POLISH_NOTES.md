# LeadLens CareOS v1.0 Final Polish

This package contains the complete LeadLens CareOS codebase through the final Business Jarvis release.

## Navigation and naming updates
- Product branding updated to **LeadLens CareOS — AI Chief of Staff for Clinics**.
- Morning Brief is now the default landing page.
- The sidebar is reduced to five clear business workspaces.
- Related screens are combined into tabbed workspaces instead of separate navigation entries.
- Development terms and phase numbers are removed from the user-facing interface.

## Combined workspaces
- Patients combines patient records and patient intelligence.
- Revenue combines revenue intelligence and revenue planning.
- AI Chief of Staff includes the Chief of Staff and Business Assistant views.
- Tasks & Approvals combines approvals and execution history.
- AI Team combines collaboration, executive council and daily meeting.
- Business Data combines data, business knowledge and system activity.

No underlying features or final-phase services were removed.
