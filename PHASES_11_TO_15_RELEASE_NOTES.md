# LeadLens CareOS — Phases 11 to 15

## Phase 11
- Dynamic Business Jarvis
- Chief of Staff now reads stored business memory
- Prioritised recommendations using current clinic signals

## Phase 12
- Scenario Simulator
- Revenue Coach
- Target-gap planning and controlled-pilot decisions

## Phase 13
- AI Executive Council
- Marketing, Operations, Finance, Customer Success and Sales viewpoints
- Chief of Staff synthesis

## Phase 14
- Daily Management Meeting
- Priority agenda and owner questions
- Learn-from-outcomes memory capture

## Phase 15
- Approval-first Execution Center
- Patient recovery, renewal, corporate outreach and reminder drafts
- Creates tasks, approvals and stored execution drafts
- Does not send externally without credentials and approval

## Important limitation
This is a testable Business Jarvis MVP. Gmail, Calendar, WhatsApp and payment sending remain disabled until real credentials and provider integrations are configured.
